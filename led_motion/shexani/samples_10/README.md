# Shexani — 10 concept samples (936×260, 6 s each)

`shexani_10_samples_reel.mp4` plays all ten with a number badge; `sample_01…10.mp4` are the clean clips.
Everything important sits in x = 140…796 (up to 140 px may be hidden on either side of the installed screen).

1. 3D window — off-axis ("naked-eye 3D") box; the X and real banknotes fly out past the yellow frame.
2. The door above the door — the fascia splits at the X like the sliding door below, onto a gold vault.
3. The invisible screen (night) — pure black, nothing touches the edges; coins orbit the logo in depth.
4. Flip-disc coin wall — ~5,700 two-sided discs (Yellow/Ink) flip in waves: $ € £ ₺ → wordmark.
5. Two arrows, one X — dinar and dollar arrive on the two arrows, swap sides, arrows become the logo.
6. Newton's cradle of coins — exchange told by physics; periodic, so the loop is seamless.
7. UV test — counterfeit-lamp sweep; fibres, rosette, logo and "پارەی ڕەسەن ✓" fluoresce, with afterglow.
8. One line around the world — New York, London, Paris, Istanbul, Erbil Citadel in one unbroken line.
9. The money press — a blank sheet runs under the roller and comes out as a Shexani brand note; the X is struck on as a seal.
10. The sign that knows the time — raised 3D letters lit from where the sun is; self-lit at night (time-lapse demo).

Source: Remotion + three.js (`src/`), official logo SVGs and brand colours/fonts from the brand package.
